Delete Your RestoDoc Account

RestoDoc
Last updated: April 10, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your RestoDoc account and all associated data. If you have any questions before submitting a request, see the Privacy Policy or contact us at the email below.

1. How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your RestoDoc account and all associated data, send an email to superiorfloors@gmail.com with the following:

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Body: Include the email address associated with your RestoDoc account.

We will use the email address you provide to locate your account. For security, we may ask you to confirm the request from that same address before processing.

2. What Gets Deleted

When we process your request, we permanently delete the following from our systems:

  • Account credentials — your email address, password hash, and session tokens
  • Jobs — all restoration jobs you have created, including property addresses, loss type, insurance claim details, and notes
  • Rooms — all rooms associated with your jobs
  • Photos and videos — all media you captured through the App, removed from cloud storage
  • Moisture readings — all reading values, locations, materials, and dates you entered
  • Equipment logs — all records of drying equipment placements
  • Floor plans — all sketches and annotations you created
  • Generated reports — all AI-generated damage assessment reports

3. Timeline

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete. After deletion, your data cannot be recovered.

4. Data We May Retain

We may retain limited records when required by law (for example, tax or audit records) or where necessary to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or protect against fraud. Any retained records are kept securely and are not used to re-identify or contact you.
